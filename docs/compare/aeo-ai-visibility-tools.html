How to choose

  • Readiness audits check what AI crawlers can read on your own site (robots.txt, llms.txt, schema, answer-style content). Visibility checks look at what AI answers actually say about a brand. Pick by which question you need answered, for example our AEO & AI Visibility Audit for readiness and Brand Facts Checker for brand facts.
  • From an AI agent, any of these can be called through the Apify MCP server at https://mcp.apify.com.
